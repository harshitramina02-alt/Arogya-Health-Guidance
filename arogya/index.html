Common questions

Is Arogya a doctor?

No. Arogya shares general health information and helps you prepare for a conversation with a health worker. It never diagnoses or suggests medicine doses.

Is my information stored?

This page does not keep your questions or reports. They are sent to an AI service to produce the answer, so leave out your name, ID numbers and contact details.

Can it read X-rays or MRI films?

No. It explains written reports, such as lab results and the radiologist's text report, but it does not interpret raw scan images.

Which languages does it support?

Pick a language in the chat window, or write in your own language and Arogya will reply in it.

What if it is an emergency?

Call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital straight away. Do not wait for an online answer.

When to get urgent help

Call your local emergency number right away for chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of a stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech), severe bleeding, poisoning, or thoughts of harming yourself.
